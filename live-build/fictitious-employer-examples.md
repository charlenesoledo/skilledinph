## Example 1 — SarapKart Food Orders

**Company / Employer name:** SarapKart Food Orders

**Contact person:** Marta Reyes

**Contact email:** demo@sarapkart.example

**Business type:** Online lutong-bahay meal orders and small catering trays

**About the business:** SarapKart takes daily meal orders through chat. An AI assistant drafts order confirmations and customer replies, but a human must check totals, delivery details, and tone before anything is sent.

**Job title:** Order & Reply Checker

**Workflow step where a human is needed:** Before an AI-drafted order confirmation or customer reply is sent to the buyer.

**Why a human is needed:** The AI writes fast, but it sometimes makes mistakes in totals, confuses delivery landmarks, and sounds too stiff in Taglish. A local human catches these before they reach the customer.

**Main tasks:**
- Check order totals, item quantities, and delivery fees.
- Verify delivery time, address notes, and landmarks.
- Check customer replies for friendly, natural Taglish tone.
- Flag orders with allergy notes or special instructions.

**Output the worker checks:** Order confirmations, delivery summaries, and short customer chat replies.

**Work setup:** Online / work from home, phone-first

**Tools needed:** Smartphone, Messenger or chat inbox, Google Sheets order list

**Reserved slots / openings:** 3

**Start date:** 2026-12-05

**Deadline to fill:** 2026-12-01

**Good example:** "Kumusta po! Confirm ko lang po ang order ninyo: 2 tapsilog at 1 lumpiang gulay. Total po ay ₱385 kasama ang delivery. Darating po ito ng 11:30 AM sa may blue gate, tapat ng barangay hall. Salamat po!"

**Bad example:** "Your order is 2 tapsilog and 1 lumpiang gulay. Total is ₱358. It will arrive at 11:30. Pay now." — total is wrong, tone is stiff, and the delivery landmark is missing.

**Skills this job needs most:**
- Price & Total Accuracy — target 85
- Taglish Tone Judgment — target 80
- Reading Comprehension — target 75

**Employer passing score:** 80

**Notes for the employer review step:** Approve the module only if the practice items use real order-checking situations: wrong totals, missing landmarks, and stiff replies.

## Example 2 — LakbayLocal Travel

**Company / Employer name:** LakbayLocal Travel

**Contact person:** Jonas Cruz

**Contact email:** demo@lakbaylocal.example

**Business type:** Small travel agency for domestic tours and family trips

**About the business:** LakbayLocal uses an AI assistant to draft travel itineraries and booking summaries. A human must check dates, names, times, and transfer details before the itinerary is sent to travellers.

**Job title:** Itinerary Detail Checker

**Workflow step where a human is needed:** Before an AI-drafted itinerary or booking summary is emailed to the traveller.

**Why a human is needed:** One wrong date, misspelled passenger name, or wrong pickup time can ruin a trip. The AI can draft quickly, but a careful human must verify the details against the booking notes.

**Main tasks:**
- Check flight dates, departure times, and airport names.
- Verify passenger name spelling against the booking note.
- Check hotel check-in and check-out dates.
- Confirm pickup points, transfer times, and contact instructions.
- Flag missing details before the itinerary is sent.

**Output the worker checks:** Travel itineraries, booking summaries, and traveller reminder messages.

**Work setup:** Online / work from home

**Tools needed:** Smartphone or laptop, email, spreadsheet, sample booking PDFs (demo files only)

**Reserved slots / openings:** 2

**Start date:** 2026-12-10

**Deadline to fill:** 2026-12-05

**Good example:** "Passenger: Ana Dela Peña. Flight: Manila to Cebu, Dec 12, 8:05 AM, NAIA Terminal 2. Hotel check-in: Dec 12, 2:00 PM. Pickup: hotel lobby, Dec 13, 7:30 AM." — all details match the booking note.

**Bad example:** "Passenger: Anna Dela Pena. Flight: Manila to Cebu, Dec 13, 8:05 AM. Hotel check-in: Dec 12." — passenger name is misspelled and the flight date does not match the hotel check-in.

**Skills this job needs most:**
- Detail Accuracy — target 85
- Reading Comprehension — target 80
- Prompt Judgment — target 75

**Employer passing score:** 80

**Notes for the employer review step:** Approve the module only if the final test includes date mismatches, name-spelling checks, and pickup-time errors.
